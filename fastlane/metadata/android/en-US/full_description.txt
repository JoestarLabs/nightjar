Nightjar is a Material 3 Expressive lock timer application for Android designed to help manage device screen time with intuitive controls and gentle warnings.

Features:

- Expressive Zen Timer Dial: A clean, interactive circular dial with a wavy progress ring to easily adjust and track active countdowns.
- Duration Picker Sheet: A playful, Material 3 bottom sheet for precise hour, minute, and second configurations.
- Sunset Warning Suite: Fullscreen warning overlays (Fluid Liquid, Material Shapes, Snowflakes, and Classic Wave) with an optional chime to gently alert when time is almost up.
- Commitment Mode: A setting that locks the timer so it cannot be stopped accidentally once started, with a slide-to-unlock emergency override.
